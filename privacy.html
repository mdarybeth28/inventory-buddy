Inventory Buddy — Privacy Policy

About Inventory Buddy

Inventory Buddy is a website that helps school cafeteria staff count and track food inventory without paper. It was built by Mary Davis, a school cafeteria worker in Tennessee, after hearing at a work meeting that paper inventory was a pain point for just about everybody there.

Privacy, in plain words

What Inventory Buddy stores: your account email and a password (the password is stored scrambled — nobody, including Mary, can read it), and your inventory: product names, quantities, storage locations, and count/delivery dates — plus, only for a school that turns that setting on, the per-case prices that school enters itself for its own inventory values.

What it never stores: student names or student records of any kind; meal accounts, balances, or eligibility information; payment information, bank details, or meal-money records; anything about anybody who is not your own school's account holder.

Who can see your inventory: only your school's account. Every account is walled off from every other account — signing in shows your school's shelves and nothing else's. Your data is never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never used for anything except running your inventory.

Where it lives: Inventory Buddy runs on GitHub Pages with its database at Supabase, an established U.S. database provider. Your inventory sits behind your password-protected login.

Leaving: stop any time — there is nothing to cancel during the pilot. Email Mary and your account and its inventory data will be deleted.

For administrators

Inventory counts are products on shelves — no student information is involved, ever. The pilot is free, there is nothing to install, and it runs in a normal web browser. If your school stops using Inventory Buddy, its account and inventory data can be deleted on request.
